LD-score regression is a widely used heritability and genetic correlation estimator in complex trait genetics. The advantage of this method is that it can estimate both these parameter using just GWAS summary statistics, which are usually publically available. However, the derivation of LDSC is not straightforward, which is why I wanted to make a blog post to explain how it works, and highlight some of its assumptions. This is a simplified explaination of Sasha Gusev's [excellent derivation], which the curious reader can refer to for a more rigorous proof. 

[excellent derivation]: http://gusevlab.org/projects/hsq/ldsc_deriv.pdf
